Gourmet condiments

Majic Jar

Small-batch jars, big conversations.

Majic Jar reimagines everyday condiments, preserves, relishes and sauces through a contemporary gourmet lens — while staying rooted in seasonal ingredients and nostalgic tastes. From Banana Rum Jam and Gondhoraj Relish to Guava Chilli Spread, seasonal mango collections and handcrafted pickles, every jar is made in small batches by hand.

Drawing on Indian kitchens, global food journeys and café culture, Majic Jar is built to elevate simple meals into memorable ones — and to give curious food lovers a reason to explore, pair and experiment.

Mountain-sourced ingredients

Parvati Pantry

An ode to the feminine energy of nature.

Named for Parvati, goddess of the mountains and the force of creation, nourishment and balance, Parvati Pantry curates artisanal ingredients sourced directly from India's high country — from the hills of Meghalaya and the valleys of Kashmir to the fertile landscapes of Uttarakhand.

Raw unprocessed honey, Lakadong turmeric, black rice, Munsiyari rajma and more — each product is chosen to preserve indigenous flavour and support the farming communities that grow it, built around honest sourcing rather than shortcuts.

British-style fish & chips

Frydays Fish n Chip Co.

A pub-style tradition, delivered fresh.

Frydays was born from a simple question: why was it so hard to find proper fish and chips in India? Built on a love for London's pub-style tradition and Bengal's own deep love of fish, Frydays set out to close that gap — one proper fish supper at a time.

Fresh fish, battered and fried to order, served with homemade tartar sauce, mushy peas, crisp fries and a splash of malt vinegar — a delivery-first brand built on comfort, memory and the joy of sharing a good meal.
